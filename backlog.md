# Backlog

Ideas and follow-ups, roughly in priority order.

- Sound: effects (building, hits, arrows, horn when troops approach) and a mute button on the title screen.
- Save and resume a game in progress.
- Difficulty setting for the computer player (and remove its free income, see tech-debt.md).
- Fastest-victory board is a single local number; show per-map-seed results or a replay seed so a map can be replayed.
- Choose or re-roll the map (seed) before starting.
- Minimap and "jump to idle builder / next attack" shortcuts.
- Tutorial: a guided first five minutes instead of the how-to-play text.
- Keyboard and mouse shortcuts for desktop (WASD pan, number keys for the build tray).
- Unit selection and direct orders (rally points for barracks, targeting for siege).
- Fog of war.
- Screenshot test of the scene in a headless browser once one is available.
- Zoom-to-fit and "reset view" buttons; auto-hide the build tray and context panel while panning so the map gets the whole screen.
- Optional "ink" outline pass (back-face hull or depth-edge) on units and buildings for even better readability against busy ground.
- Colour-blind friendly team marks: a shape or pattern on units and roofs as well as blue versus red.
- Strategic zoom: below a certain camera height, swap units for bold team-coloured pips so a big army still reads at a glance.
- Rotate the camera in 90 degree steps (needs the light bands re-tuned per heading, see tech-debt.md).
- A "Look" setting on the title screen: flat (default), tilt-shift miniature, and a high-contrast mode.
- Resource tiles that regrow (trees) after being cleared, or a forester building.
- Show how much is left in a node when hovering it (a tooltip with harvests remaining).
- Per-kind harvest yield and tool upgrades so rock gets faster to clear later in a game.
- Let people visibly queue and step aside on a shared building rim.
- Show all three lanes faintly on the map (not just the one a barracks would use), with a toggle, so the layout is clear before building.
- Let the player re-assign an existing barracks to another lane without rebuilding it.
- Lane objectives: towers placed along each lane that must fall before the next, or a creep wave that follows each lane.
- Jungle camps between the lanes with a reward (iron, a free officer) for clearing them.
- Show a unit's production as a tiny icon of the thing being made beside the progress bar.
- Long-press a building or tile for quick actions (raze, upgrade, march) next to the build menu.
- Remember the last building picked per category and offer it first in the menu.
- Drag a building card from the sheet straight onto the map.
- A tiny "what is this?" label over buildings when zoomed out, since the glyphs are the only cue.
- Show a building's category colour as a legend in the help screen.
